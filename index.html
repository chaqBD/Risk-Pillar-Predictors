Risk Pillar Predictors

Starting engine…

Upload monthly risk-pillar scores by country, train up to 10 machine-learning models, and see how accurately each one predicts the most recent months. Everything runs in your browser, so your data never leaves your computer.

1Upload risk pillar data

A CSV with one row per country per month.

What format does the file need?

Columns: Country, Period (e.g. 2025-01), then one numeric column per risk pillar. Each country needs at least 13 months of history before the test period.
